What you are looking at

The seed fixes the whole game before the first move: the starting board and every piece to come. A beam search plays it in your browser, keeping only the best boards at each step, and tries to clear the goal with the pieces it has.

  1. Watch. Press play or step with the arrow keys. The Beam view shows every board the search kept.
  2. Change. Pick a preset, type a seed, or narrow the beam width and watch the search start to lose.
  3. Measure. Measure win rate solves many seeds with your settings and compares the result with the reference machine.
