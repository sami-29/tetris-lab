### Hard violations (documented standard)

- **Standards 1: `web/src/styles/shell.css:1565` (`#tour-ring`).**
  - The header of `tokens.css` allows "no drop shadows except on the drawer and the tooltip" and a "2 px radius at most".
  - The ring dims the page with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 200vmax …` and has `border-radius: 3px`.
  - Fix: use a 2px radius, and dim with a backdrop element or a clip-path (untried). Alternatively, add the ring as a documented exception.
- **Standards 2: `web/src/styles/shell.css:1590` (`#tour`).**
  - The tour card has a `0 18px 48px` drop shadow, and the card is not one of the two listed exceptions.
  - Whether it counts as a third floating thing is a judgement call. If it does, list it in the header.
- **Standards 3: `web/src/styles/shell.css:590` (`.guide-steps li::before`).**
  - The rule sets `font: 600 10px / 1 var(--mono)`, a literal size below the 11px floor and off the six-step scale.
  - Fix: use `var(--fs-micro)`.
- **Standards 4: the header comment of `web/src/styles/tokens.css`.**
  - The header was not updated for the new exceptions: the tour shadows and the page-wide scrollbar and selection styling.
  - `::selection` hard-codes amber as `rgba(224,163,62,…)` instead of using a token. The existing rules already repeated that pattern.

### Judgement calls (smells)

- **Standards 5: possible Duplicated Code, `web/src/main.ts`.**
  - The resize handler and `setMode` both contain the same block: `if (playback) { playback.draw(); painted = …; paintStrip(…) } else view.draw(shownBoard)`. Extract a `redrawBoard()` helper.
  - The coercion `dataset.mode === "measure" ? "measure" : "watch"` appears in two places. Extract a `modeOf(button)` helper.
- **Standards 6: possible Duplicated Code, `web/src/ui/playground.ts:215-221` and `:276-279`.** Both field builders now build a `div.field` wrapper. Share one small helper for it.
- **Standards 7: possible Speculative Generality.**
  - In `web/src/ui/tree-panel.ts`, `layersPanel?` is optional and `layersButton` can be null, but `main.ts` always passes both.
  - In `web/src/ui/tour.ts`, `TourOptions.stops` can be injected, but no test or caller passes it.
  - Fix: make the panel and the button required, and drop `stops`.
- **Standards 8: no coverage for `tour.ts`.** It carries its own focus-return, key-capture and docking logic. No test or shell-check step names the tour.

Clean: no "removed code" comments, no versioned names and no migration code. `PLAYGROUND_TEXT` is untouched, and the tour copy quotes no numbers.

## Spec

The reviewer viewed the before screenshot and the after screenshots at 1920, 1440 and 390 wide. Clutter, the tour and the stated purpose are met, and the change at 1920 wide is a large improvement. The scrollbar requirement is only partly met.

### (a) Missing or partial

- **Spec (a)1: partial.** "I hate the "overflows" and "scrollbars"".
  - Four internal scrollers remain in `shell.css`, each `overflow: auto`:
    - `.readout`, line 717;
    - `.tablewrap`, line 1249;
    - `.dbody`, line 1432;
    - `.measure-body`, line 1735.
  - No scrollbars appear in the 1920×1000 and 1440×900 screenshots.
  - Nothing proves they stay hidden at 1366×768 or 1280×720. Probe: extend `web/scripts/shell-check.mjs` to assert `scrollHeight <= clientHeight` on those four elements at 1366×768, 1280×720 and 1536×864.
- **Spec (a)2: partial.** "there's weird empty spaces".
  - Measure mode at 1440 wide:
    - The rail has a gap of about 90px between the guide text and "Take the tour".
    - The Yours column is mostly empty under "Nothing measured yet".
  - Watch mode at 1920 wide: the column-heights profile is very tall for what it shows.
- **Spec (a)3: partial.** "what's the purpose of this really".
  - The guide says what you are looking at, but not the question the Lab answers in one sentence. `PRODUCT.md` puts that question as "can the search clear N lines, and how?".
  - The tour does not open on first visit. An amber dot on the Tour button is the only sign it exists.

### (b) Not asked for

- **Spec (b)1.** These support the clutter goal, but the request did not ask for them:
  - the brand tagline was rewritten;
  - a result headline was added;
  - the inspector became a Beam / Tree / Layers tab bar;
  - a localStorage "tour seen" flag was added.

### (c) Implemented but looks wrong

- **Spec (c)1.** "overflows". At 390 wide the status bar clips its chips horizontally ("space pl…", "40 pieces"). The relevant rules are in the `@media (max-width: 560px)` block of `shell.css`, at line 2204.
- **Spec (c)2.** Guide step 3, "Switch to Measure win rate…", still shows while Measure is already open.
- **Spec (c)3.** Layers is now behind a tab. At HEAD it was a panel that was always visible (`index.html:315`). The page is calmer, but the layer table is harder to find.
